Finance philosophy
How I think about Finance

Numbers should tell stories

📐

Rigour over speed

Accurate data is the foundation of every decision. I prioritise clean numbers over fast ones — a wrong forecast costs more than a late one.

🔍

Variance as a signal

A gap between actual and budget isn't a failure — it's a question. I dig into variances to find the real operational story behind the numbers.

🤝

Finance as a partner

Finance teams that sit in back offices miss the point. The most valuable work happens when finance sits alongside operations and leadership.
